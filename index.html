Dive deeper

Paul Whiteman of the NAHT called phones an “unhelpful distraction” that raises “safety issues” but said the measures “come at a cost”; the ASCL’s Pepe Di’Iasio said leaders “cannot simply make mobile phones disappear” and that the bill for secure storage “will be very significant indeed”. The policy sits beside the under-16 social media ban due in March, and England now moves ahead of Wales, where the Senedd rejected a statutory ban, while Scotland legislates and Northern Ireland consults. The announcement rests on one outlet’s overnight report; watch for the Department for Education’s published guidance and the funding question.
