Trump told Netanyahu “Don’t” on continued Beirut strikes Tuesday and announced an Israel-Hezbollah cessation of hostilities. By Wednesday, Israel and Lebanon had signed a conditional ceasefire through US-mediated talks in Washington. The intervention was the most significant US-side de-escalation move in the regional war to date — even as Hezbollah later rejected the terms.

Israel’s defence minister said Thursday the truce grants Israel the freedom to strike Beirut.

Putin Rejects Zelensky Face-to-Face Meeting at SPIEF

Vladimir Putin on Friday rejected Volodymyr Zelensky’s face-to-face meeting proposal, saying he sees “no point” in it. The rejection closed the most significant Ukrainian diplomatic move in months. Ukraine responded by striking the Kronstadt naval base and a guided-missile corvette Saturday, plus a second large St Petersburg drone wave that Russia said it intercepted with 376 drones downed.
