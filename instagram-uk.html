Fuel Prices Rising — Petrol Up 2p to 152p

The RAC confirmed the first pump increases since the ceasefire. Average petrol hit 152p, with motorway stations already at 158p. Diesel up 1.5p to 179.5p. Monday’s $103 oil spike is still feeding through — further rises are locked in. The AA warns 160p petrol by next week. The Government is “monitoring closely” but won’t intervene.
