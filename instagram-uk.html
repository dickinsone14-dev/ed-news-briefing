Badenoch used all six questions to hammer Starmer on defence spending after former Labour defence secretary George Robertson accused the government of “corrosive complacency.” Robertson warned Britain is “under-prepared, under-insured, under-attack” and noted welfare spending is five times the defence budget. The Speaker rebuked Starmer for attacking the opposition instead of answering.
