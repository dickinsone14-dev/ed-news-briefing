Starmer Delivers State Apology for Forced Adoptions

Sir Keir Starmer delivered a state apology for the forced adoptions of 185,000 babies taken from unmarried mothers between 1949 and 1976: “The shame is not yours. The shame was never yours. The shame is ours.” A £4m package will fund records access and reunion services, though there is no compensation scheme.
