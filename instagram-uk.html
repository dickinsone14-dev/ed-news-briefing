Fuel Hits 155p Despite Oil Pullback

Petrol rose to 155p, motorway stations above 162p. Diesel at 182p. Oil has fallen 10% from Monday’s $103 peak to $93.50 — but the 7–10 day wholesale lag means pump prices keep rising until Friday. The RAC says Friday will be the peak. The AA urged drivers to delay fills until Tuesday. The 5p fuel duty freeze offers less than 3% relief at current prices.

Defence Committee Battles for Public Lakenheath Hearing

The Defence Committee meets Thursday on whether US strikes on Iran were launched from RAF Lakenheath. The MoD is pushing for a classified session. Chair Mark Francois is resisting: “The public has a right to know if British soil was used.” If classified, the key testimony stays secret. The answer could trigger a constitutional crisis — UK territory used for attacks on a country Britain is neutral toward.
