Byrne: “Rule Out Security Services Exemptions” from Hillsborough Law

On the 37th anniversary of Hillsborough, Ian Byrne pressed Starmer to guarantee no carve-outs for security services in the Hillsborough Law. The PM reiterated his commitment to delivering the law but would not give the specific guarantee, citing ongoing negotiations with victim families. Byrne was visibly frustrated by the non-answer.

Cross-Party: “Northern Ireland Can’t Heat Its Homes”

In a rare cross-community intervention, Alliance’s Sorcha Eastwood and the DUP’s Gregory Campbell both raised the heating oil crisis in Northern Ireland, where 68% of homes rely on oil. The government pledged £17 million — dismissed as “approximately £25 per household, less than a single tank fill.” The Hormuz crisis is hitting NI harder than any other region.
