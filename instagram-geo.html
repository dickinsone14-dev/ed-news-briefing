Trump Announces 10-Day Israel–Lebanon Ceasefire

Trump announced a 10-day ceasefire between Israel and Lebanon on Truth Social, effective midnight local time. Both Israel and Lebanon’s government confirmed within the hour. The truce covers all Israeli operations in Lebanon and Hezbollah rocket fire. Hezbollah said it was “studying the announcement.” Trump called it “a stepping stone to permanent peace.”

US & Iran Agree Ceasefire Extension “In Principle”

The US and Iran agreed in principle to extend the ceasefire beyond 21 April via the Pakistan back-channel. No formal document is signed. The biggest obstacle remains uranium enrichment: the US wants a 20-year suspension, Iran counters with 5 years. The “in principle” signal was enough to push oil to $93.80 and stabilise global markets.

Satellite Images: Iran Clearing Missile Launch Bases

Commercial satellite imagery shows Iran dismantling mobile missile launch systems at three sites in Khuzestan and Fars provinces. Planet Labs data shared with Reuters shows activity accelerating today. Analysts at CSIS described it as “the most significant Iranian concession posture since the ceasefire began.” The US said it is “watching carefully before drawing conclusions.”

US Clarifies Blockade — Commercial Oil Vessels Exempt

The Pentagon formally confirmed the Hormuz blockade applies only to military equipment and dual-use technology — not commercial oil tankers. Oil fell to $93.80 on the announcement. Iran called it “a face-saving reinterpretation of an illegal blockade.” The clarification aligns US policy with the compromise framework Pakistan brought to Tehran, and removes the economic rationale for Iran’s Red Sea counter-threat.

UK GDP Revised Up to 0.7% — But IMF Cuts 2026 Outlook

The ONS revised Q4 2025 UK GDP growth to 0.7% (from 0.5%), driven by stronger services and business investment. But the IMF separately cut its 2026 UK growth forecast to 1.1% from 1.4%, citing the blockade’s impact on energy costs and export demand. Economists warned: “Q4 is ancient history. Q1 2026 will tell the real story.”
