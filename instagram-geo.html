Pakistan Hands US Revised Iranian Proposal; “Not Much Time”

Pakistan today shared with the United States a revised proposal from Iran to end the war in the Middle East, a Pakistani source told Reuters, warning that the two sides “don’t have much time” to narrow their differences. Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson Esmaeil Baghaei confirmed Tehran’s views had been “conveyed to the American side through Pakistan”. President Donald Trump is expected to meet his top national security advisers tomorrow to discuss options for resuming military action, Axios reported. Trump posted on Truth Social: “the Clock is Ticking. TIME IS OF THE ESSENCE!”

ISW: Barakah Drone Strike “Likely Iranian”; Tehran Blames Riyadh

The Institute for the Study of War assesses that “likely Iranian or Iranian-backed forces” launched the three drones that struck the UAE’s Barakah Nuclear Power Plant on Sunday, with two intercepted and one starting a fire at an electrical generator outside the inner perimeter. ISW notes the westerly approach echoes Iran’s 2019 Abqaiq attack on Saudi oil facilities, deliberately obscuring responsibility. IRGC-affiliated Tasnim News Agency and Defa Press are now openly attempting to attribute the strike to Saudi Arabia. The IAEA registered “grave concern”; the UAE Foreign Ministry called the attack a “dangerous escalation”.

Katz: Israel “Awaiting Green Light” to Renew Iran War

Israeli Defence Minister Israel Katz said over the weekend that the IDF is “ready in defence and offence, and the targets are marked”, and that Israel is “awaiting a green light from the United States” to resume the war on Iran, the Times of Israel reported. Katz set the operational objectives in explicit terms: “complete the elimination of the Khamenei dynasty… return Iran to the age of darkness and stone by blowing up central energy and electricity facilities”. The renewed campaign, he said, would be “different and deadly and will add devastating blows in the most painful places”.

Israel Kills Islamic Jihad Commander in Baalbeck Strike

An Israeli airstrike near the eastern Lebanese city of Baalbeck overnight killed a commander of the Palestinian Islamic Jihad militant group, Wael Mahmoud Abd al-Halim, along with his daughter, Lebanese security sources told Reuters. Hezbollah responded by launching an explosive drone at an Iron Dome air defence position in the Galilee region of northern Israel. The IDF said it struck more than thirty Hezbollah sites in southern Lebanon over the past twenty-four hours. Lebanon’s health ministry put the cumulative civilian death toll at 2,988 since 2 March.

Ukraine Hits Moscow Refinery, Sheremetyevo Runway; 51 Flights Diverted

The Institute for the Study of War confirms that Ukrainian forces struck the Angstrem Semiconductor Plant in Zelenograd — producing microelectronics for high-precision weapons — alongside the Moscow Oil Refinery in Kapotnya Raion and the Solnechnogorsk and Volodarsk oil pumping stations. A separate strike triggered a runway fire at Sheremetyevo International Airport, forcing the diversion of 51 flights. Russian milbloggers are openly calling for retaliation with tactical nuclear weapons; Kremlin spokesperson Dmitry Peskov said Russia “cannot be threatened as a nuclear power”.
